# Design Preferences

For future apps, when the product type fits, prefer a visual direction similar to Quick Extract:

- Beautiful, easy to read, eye-catching, and smooth.
- Modern dark galaxy / star-sky atmosphere with glassy panels.
- Cyan, violet, silver-white, and subtle warm accents.
- Clear utility-first layout: polished, but not noisy or overly decorative.
- Comfortable density for large / 4K displays.
- Smooth interaction states and restrained motion.

Use this style when it supports the app's purpose. For apps where another style is clearly more appropriate, keep the same quality bar but adapt the visual theme.
